Call the dispatch line

The fastest way to reach Evergreen Water Damage Pros is to call (303) 952-8295. The line is answered 24/7, including weekends and holidays. Calls may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

This site has no contact forms. Emergencies and quotes go by phone so the dispatcher can ask follow-up questions and start the right response. For non-urgent questions, you can email the address in the footer, though a call is usually faster.

What to have ready

Dispatch can help even if you do not have every detail.

  • The street address where the water is.
  • The source of the water, if known, and whether it has been stopped.
  • Which rooms are wet and about how long it has been wet.
  • Whether the water looks clean, cloudy or contaminated.
  • Anything electrical that is near the water.

Frequently asked questions

Why are there no contact forms?

Water damage is time sensitive, and a conversation gets answers faster than a form. Dispatch asks the questions that matter and starts the response while you are on the line.

Is the line really answered 24/7?

Yes, every day of the year. Same-day response is offered when available, and the dispatcher tells you what is realistic.

Do you give prices over the phone?

Scope and price are explained before work starts. Costs depend on the loss, so dispatch explains the factors and what the next step is.
