Water Damage Restoration in El Pinal, Evergreen, CO

The El Pinal area saw a water main break, exposed sewer lines and a boil order after the June 2026 storm. This page explains what that means if water or silt ended up inside your house.

What happened in the El Pinal area in June 2026

The Canyon Courier calls it "the El Pinal neighborhood" and "the El Pinal area of Evergreen," and that is how this page describes it. No boundaries are drawn here, because which census area it falls in has not been verified. If you are unsure whether your street is covered, call and ask whether your address is in the service area.

According to reports from Denver7, the Canyon Courier and CBS Colorado, a storm on June 24 to 25, 2026 left the area with a water main break near Troublesome Gulch and El Pinal that affected nearly 200 customers. Erosion exposed sewer lines, and two private bridges on Troublesome Gulch washed away. Rainfall reports varied, from an official gauge reading of about 3.5 inches to unofficial estimates of up to about 6 inches over roughly two hours.

Not every home took water. CBS Colorado reported that water from Troublesome Creek entered some homes, so check your own walls, floors and crawl space instead of assuming.

Boil order versus restoration job

Evergreen Metropolitan District issued a boil water order for the El Pinal area after the storm. A boil order is a drinking water notice from the district, and it is not a restoration job by itself. The Courier reported that a second storm that evening refilled the repair excavation with sediment, and the order lasted about 48 hours.

Here is where it can reach your floors. Sediment in lines, a drained system and sewer problems can all push water into a house in ways a normal leak does not. Silty or muddy water inside the home is treated as contaminated until proven otherwise, which puts it in the same category of work as flood damage restoration and not a simple clean water leak.

If a drain is backing up, see sewage backup cleanup.

Streets named in the district's boil order

According to the list Denver7 printed from Evergreen Metropolitan District, the order covered streets such as El Pinal Rd, S. Saddle Rd, Arena Dr, Stagecoach Blvd, Carriage Loop Dr, Troublesome Gulch Rd, Russell Gulch Rd and Mooshadow Ln, along with many others. Being on that list means your tap water was under a notice. It does not mean your house took on water.

If your street appears there and you found wet floors, tell the dispatcher your street name. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified, so check your address on FEMA's Flood Map Service Center.

Whose problem is it: the district's side or the house side

Evergreen Metropolitan District handles the water main and distribution system. A break in the street or a district sewer issue is the district's to repair, and Denver7 reported three sewer system issues and one water distribution issue under repair. Past your connection, wet drywall and soaked subfloor are a private drying job.

Calling the right party first saves hours. Report street-side trouble to the district, and call for water mitigation when the water is inside your walls. For storm-driven intrusion, storm water damage explains how that loss differs from a plumbing failure.

Access: washed-out bridges and gulch roads

Two private bridges on Troublesome Gulch were washed away, according to the reports. If your driveway crossed one, or a gulch road is damaged near you, restoration professionals need to know before anyone drives out. Equipment such as extraction units and air movers is heavy, and a truck cannot use a bridge that is not there.

When you call, describe how a vehicle reaches your door, whether a crossing is out, and whether a neighbor's road is the only way in. That detail lets the dispatcher confirm what is possible for your address. Safety before anyone arrives

Safety with silt, mud and sewage

  • Do not wade through muddy or standing water, and keep children and pets out of it.
  • Do not touch breakers or switches while standing in water. If the panel is wet or you cannot reach it dry, call your electric utility or 911.
  • Do not run household fans or an ordinary shop vacuum on sewage or contaminated water. They spread what you are trying to contain.
  • Shut off the home's water only if you can reach the valve safely.
  • Photograph the water line, damaged materials and any silt when it is safe to do so.
  • If there is immediate danger, such as a gas smell or rising water, leave and call 911.

Documenting for your insurer

Photos, moisture readings from a meter and an itemized scope of work all help show what happened and when. Coverage depends on the cause and the terms of your policy, so check your policy and ask your insurer. The Colorado Division of Insurance advises contacting your insurer early and sending an inventory of damaged items in one package.

The cause matters. Flood, a plumbing failure and sewer backup are often handled under different coverage, and a sewer backup endorsement is a separate item to look for. Nothing here promises that any claim will be approved.

How the drying work is done

Crews measure first with moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras, then remove water with extraction units and set air movers and LGR dehumidifiers. ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published reference standard for water damage restoration, and you can ask any company whether its work follows it. For a deeper look at the cleanup stage, see water damage cleanup.

If damp materials stay wet, mold can become a question. Ask whether the provider offers mold remediation and whether the state licenses mold work. Health questions belong with a physician.

Frequently asked questions

Does a boil water order mean I have water damage?

No. A boil order is a drinking water notice from Evergreen Metropolitan District. Water damage only exists if water or silt got into floors, walls or a crawl space.

If you do find wet materials, tell the dispatcher what you saw and when.

Is muddy water inside my house safe to clean myself?

Silty or muddy water is treated as contaminated until proven otherwise. Stay out of it, keep pets and children away, and avoid household fans and ordinary vacuums. A professional can test and extract it properly.

Can I call about water damage in El Pinal after hours?

Yes, you can call at any hour. The dispatch line handles urgent calls and confirms what is possible for your address, including how a vehicle can reach your street.

Who do I call about a water main or sewer problem in the street?

Evergreen Metropolitan District handles its own mains and sewer lines. Trouble inside the house, past your connection, is a private drying and cleanup job that you can call about here.
