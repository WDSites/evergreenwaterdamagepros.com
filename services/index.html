Emergency pages carry the word emergency for a reason. Those are the services people call when water is still moving, and crews are reachable around the clock. The planned services, water damage repair and mold remediation, usually follow a drying job, although a mold call can come with no recent flood at all.

How the services work together

Most water losses use more than one service in order. A burst pipe may start with removal, move to mitigation and cleanup, and end with repair. A basement flood may need removal, flood cleanup, drying and then mold checks before the walls close. Dispatch sorts this out on the first call so you do not pay for the same step twice.

The standards behind the work are published by the IICRC. ANSI/IICRC S500 covers water damage restoration and S520 covers mold remediation. We name them so you can ask any company you consider whether its crews work to the same standards. We do not claim a certification on behalf of any company.

What every service has in common

Each job starts with the scope of work and how the price is set, explained before work begins. Crews use moisture meters, thermal imaging cameras, air movers, LGR dehumidifiers and extraction units, and they record readings so the job can be checked. The network dispatches licensed and insured restoration professionals to Evergreen and the nearby towns of Conifer, Indian Hills, Morrison, Golden and Idledale.

Some things are outside this service. Restoration is not waterproofing, and a basement that keeps taking on water may need drainage work from another trade. Fire and smoke damage is not offered here. If your situation does not fit a card, call and ask.

Frequently asked questions

Which service do I need if I am not sure?

Call dispatch and describe what you see. The dispatcher asks about the source, how long it has been wet and what materials are affected, then routes you to the right crew.

Do you offer all six services in every town?

Yes. Services are the same across Evergreen, Conifer, Indian Hills, Morrison, Golden and Idledale, though local conditions change the work. Each town page explains those differences.

Is emergency service available around the clock?

Yes. The dispatch line runs 24/7. Same-day response is offered when available, and the dispatcher explains what is realistic for your address.

How is pricing explained?

Scope and price are explained before work starts. The cost depends on water category, affected area, materials, drying days and rebuild scope, and no one can quote it responsibly without seeing the loss.
