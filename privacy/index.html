Overview

This policy covers evergreenwaterdamagepros.com and the phone line listed on it. Evergreen Water Damage Pros is a call-only service. The site has no forms, no accounts and no checkout, so it collects very little directly.

Phone calls

When you call (303) 952-8295 we handle the information you share on the call, such as your name, address, phone number and a description of the damage. We use it to dispatch a crew, to explain scope and price and to follow up on your request. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

We share call details with the restoration professionals who may serve your address, because they need it to respond. We do not describe or promise anything beyond that on this page.

Information collected by the website

Like most websites, the server and its hosting provider may log technical information such as your IP address, browser type and pages visited. We use it to keep the site running and secure.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and displays maps from Google Maps. Those services may receive your IP address and browser details when a page loads. Their own privacy policies govern what they do with it.

Cookies

The site does not set advertising cookies of its own. Third-party services such as the map embeds may set their own cookies, and you can control cookies in your browser settings.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information from a call by contacting us at the email address in the footer. We will respond as soon as practical. You can also decline to be recorded by ending the call and reaching us through email.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. The current version is always on this page.
